The rule before downsampling
Choose the downsampling factor M. The new sampling rate is fs/M and the new Nyquist limit is (fs/M)/2. Everything above that limit must be removed by the LPF first.
Remember: New rate = fs / M. New Nyquist = half of that. The LPF cutoff should be at or below this new Nyquist.
